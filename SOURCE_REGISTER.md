# Source Register and Research Notes

**Research cut-off: 27 October 2026**  
This register lists public sources used to build the CPS v2 casebook. “Primary” means a court/regulator/company filing or official government record. Corporate statements are primary for what the company said, not independent proof that the statement is correct. News reporting is best used for timelines and attributed positions. Academic abstracts support only what the abstract describes.

## A. Primary legal, regulator and government sources

1. **Supreme Court of India: Tata Consultancy Services Ltd. v. Cyrus Investments Pvt. Ltd. (26 March 2021)** — court judgment on Tata–Mistry appeals; use for procedural history and actual legal holdings. Indian Kanoon hosts the full text: https://indiankanoon.org/doc/5416696/
2. **US Securities and Exchange Commission: Satyam fraud charges (5 April 2011)** — SEC charged Satyam with overstating revenue, income and cash over five years by more than US$1 billion. This source describes the SEC’s complaint/charges. https://www.sec.gov/news/press/2011/2011-81.htm
3. **Ministry of Corporate Affairs / Press Information Bureau: Satyam case chronology (2009)** — official summary of the attempted Maytas acquisition, disclosure, board replacement, bidding and investigations. https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=56471
4. **MCA / PIB: IL&FS board supersession (1 October 2018)** — official statement on government application to NCLT and replacement board. https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1548136&lang=2&reg=48
5. **Reserve Bank of India: Yes Bank reconstruction scheme (6 March 2020)** — primary source on moratorium and reconstruction after deteriorating financial position. https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=49479
6. **MCA / PIB: SFIO investigation of entities connected to PNB fraud (16 March 2018)** — official announcement of investigation into 107 companies and seven LLPs connected with named groups. https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1524869&lang=2&reg=3
7. **MCA / PIB: recovery update linked to Nirav Modi bankruptcy proceedings (25 August 2020)** — official account of recovery tranche and references to US bankruptcy examiner report; investigation/court process language must be kept precise. https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1648579&lang=2&reg=3
8. **Delhi High Court order re DHFL (31 May 2024)** — court order recounting CBI charge-sheet allegations; allegations must not be reported as final conviction. https://indiankanoon.org/doc/59068148/
9. **Godrej Industries stock-exchange filing / family settlement agreement (30 April 2024)** — primary company disclosure of the family settlement and brand/non-compete arrangements. https://www.godrejindustries.com/uploads/GIL_Reg30_FSAF_8a798cfd04.pdf
10. **Infosys investigation disclosure on anonymous whistleblower allegations (2020)** — company’s published account of independent investigation outcome; primary for the company’s disclosed outcome, not an independent court ruling. https://www.infosys.com/newsroom/press-releases/documents/audit-committee-finds-no-evidence-financial-impropriety.pdf
11. **Infosys Chairman’s 2019 address on whistleblower process** — company account of chronology and review process. https://www.infosys.com/investors/news-events/analyst-meet/2019/india/nandan-nilekani-speech.pdf
12. **India Code: Companies Act, 2013** — statutory source. Verify current amendments and rules before giving a legal answer. https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/2114?view_type=browse
13. **SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements regulations / board duties and vigil mechanism** — use current consolidated regulations when advising on listed entities. The linked document may be a versioned copy; check SEBI’s latest regulation page before relying on a provision. https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/mar-2023/1679546820987.pdf
14. **Government of India Labour Codes announcement (21 November 2025)** — Union government announced the four Labour Codes effective from this date. For a live dispute, check current official rules and State notifications. https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?LID=1&PRID=2192463&RegID=3&lang=2&reg=48
15. **India Code: POSH Act, 2013** — official statutory text. https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/17057?sam_handle=123456789%2F2490
16. **Ministry of Women and Child Development: legislation index including POSH Act/Rules/Handbook** — official source directory. https://www.wcd.gov.in/documents/legislations
17. **Institute of Secretariat Training & Management: POSH Act guidebook (updated 2026)** — official government training guide; use alongside the statute and rules. https://istm.gov.in/home/posh_act

## Evidence-use rules

- Prefer original judgments, regulator actions, official filings and statutory text for what was formally decided.
- For contested cases, summarize each side’s documented position and subsequent procedural outcome.
- Never cite a press release as independent verification of its own claims.
- If an item is only a paywalled academic abstract, do not invent the full article’s findings.
- Treat current laws, appointments, settlements and corporate status as perishable; research them again at answer time.
- Do not use anonymous social media posts, gossip blogs or scraped “insider” lists as evidence of individual wrongdoing.
