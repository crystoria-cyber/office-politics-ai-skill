## The Problem With Workplace AI Advice Today

When you ask any AI model for help navigating a difficult workplace situation, you typically get one of two useless answers:

> ❌ **The HR Platitude:** *"Schedule a 1-on-1 and share your feelings openly."*
> *(In a political office, this can get you sidelined or labelled "difficult.")*

> ❌ **The Machiavellian Trap:** Vague advice about "building alliances" that ignores ethics, law, and long-term career health.

> ❌ **The Ethnocentric Blindspot:** Advice built for Silicon Valley's flat hierarchy that completely fails in founder-led companies, family businesses, or emerging markets.

**Office Politics AI Skill fixes all three.** It is a structured skill file you load into any AI model to transform it into an evidence-aware, ethical organizational strategy coach.

## Real Examples: Why This Matters

### 🔴 Situation 1: Your colleague took credit for your work in a leadership meeting

**Without this skill:** The AI says *"Talk to your manager and explain what happened."*

**With this skill:** The AI gives you a structured response:
- **Facts vs. hypotheses:** Separates what actually happened from assumed motives
- **Evidence strategy:** Tells you to build a work-contribution log *before* the next meeting, not after
- **Script:** *"In yesterday's review, the analysis was described as the team's work. I built the model and recommendation, with data support from X. Can we update the project note so the contribution record is accurate? For the next review, I'd like to present the model since I own its assumptions."*
- **Risk check:** Warns you NOT to counter-gossip, exaggerate, or make the colleague fail publicly

---

### 🔴 Situation 2: Your VP hasn't responded to your proposal in 2 weeks

**Without this skill:** *"They're probably busy. Follow up politely."*

**With this skill:**
- **Hypothesis table:** Lists 4 plausible reasons (overloaded / waiting for budget / unclear on ask / unconvinced) — forces you to stop assuming the worst
- **Decision-ready brief template:** Rewrites your follow-up as: *"The decision needed is whether to run the November pilot. The main upside is [evidence]; the trade-off is [risk/cost]. Would you prefer I (A) refine the business case, (B) reduce scope, or (C) revisit in the next planning cycle?"*
- **Success signal:** Tells you exactly what a "yes" vs. "polite delay" looks like

---

### 🔴 Situation 3: You work at a family-owned company where the owner overrides every manager

**Without this skill:** *"Have a conversation with HR about role clarity."* (There is no real HR.)

**With this skill:**
- **Diagnoses** the "Lala company" pattern across 7 observable dimensions (decision centralization, delegation reality, family roles, information asymmetry, people systems, exceptions, professionalization stage)
- **Provides India-specific context** including legal frameworks (4 Labour Codes, POSH Act, SEBI LODR) with official government links
- **Script for the owner override:** *"I want to make sure I follow the approval process correctly. For purchases of this type, should I treat the manager's approval as final up to a defined amount, or should the owner approve each case?"*
- **References real cases:** Tata-Mistry, Infosys Sikka, Godrej settlement — as learning patterns, not gossip

---

### 🔴 Situation 4: You're being set up to fail — impossible deadlines, moving goalposts

**Without this skill:** *"Document everything and talk to HR."*

**With this skill:** Identifies this as Pattern #11 (Overloading) and Pattern #17 (Moving Goalposts) from its 26-pattern library, and gives you:
- **Capacity presentation script:** *"Both requests are important. With current capacity, I can complete A by Friday or B by Friday, but not both to the required standard. Which one should take priority, and what should move?"*
- **Version control advice:** How to lock scope and criteria in writing before delivery
- **Escalation ladder:** 4-level proportionate escalation path (Clarify → Align → Facilitate → Formal)

### The POLITIC Framework (Built Into Every Response)

When you describe a workplace challenge, Office Politics AI Skill works through a structured process:

```
P — Pin down the outcome (what decision is actually needed?)
O — Observe the system (formal org chart vs. informal power map)
L — Locate stakeholders and their real interests
I — Identify ethical influence paths and options
T — Talk, negotiate, and document
I — Inspect outcomes and iterate
C — Check ethics before finalizing
```

## Ethics First

Office Politics AI Skill is designed with hard ethical boundaries built into every response. It will **never** help you:

- Lie about facts, offers, approvals, or endorsements
- Smear, sabotage, blackmail, or covertly undermine a colleague
- Conduct unauthorized surveillance or access private messages
- Retaliate against whistleblowers or complainants
- Evade legitimate governance, safety, or compliance controls

The goal is **durable trust and legitimate organizational outcomes** — not winning at any cost.

For serious matters (discrimination, harassment, retaliation, fraud, safety risks), Office Politics AI Skill
always directs you to appropriate formal channels: HR, compliance, ombuds, union
representatives, or qualified legal counsel.
